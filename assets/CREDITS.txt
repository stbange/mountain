Taggart Lake elevations are derived from public-domain U.S. Geological Survey 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) data, 1 arc-second product, tile n44w111. Mountain contributors resampled the survey and identified the lake surface. The hike, vegetation and seasonal appearance are composed for the game.
Source: https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/1/TIFF/current/n44w111/USGS_1_n44w111.tif

The atlas photographs are captures of Mountain. The regional landforms, lunar craters and fictional worlds are procedural interpretations by Mountain contributors. The landmark and treasure models, atlas photographs, destination shelters, spring baths, winter garden, lookout creatures, geological and mechanical sightings, surf, wind, thunder, suit resonance and pipe chimes are original CC BY 4.0 artwork and sound. The Earth destination names identify the landscapes that inspired them.
